# Problem statement

**Status: draft.** The playbook's problem description is a paraphrase. Before submission, confirm the exact SIH26047 wording on the official SIH portal and record the source here.

Government hospital outpatient departments serve many patients in a short time. Patients describe their concerns in different languages and in unstructured ways, while queues may not provide a synchronized token or wait position. OPD Saathi aims to collect a structured patient history before the consultation and help staff manage the queue. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
