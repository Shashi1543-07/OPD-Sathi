# Safety

OPD Saathi collects history only. It must not diagnose, name a likely disease, suggest medicines or doses, give treatment advice, or reassure patients. Patients see neutral language; staff receive explainable alerts when a rule indicates that a patient may need earlier attention. Uncertainty must escalate, never lower urgency.

Question banks, red-flag rules, and routing rules are drafts requiring clinician review. Use synthetic data only. Keep patient confirmation and clinician editing in the flow. See the safety framing in Part B6 of `AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md`.
