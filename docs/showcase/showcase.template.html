Built to be trusted in a hospital

AI helps. The system never depends on it.

History only, never a diagnosis

No disease named, no medicine or dose suggested. AI text that advises or diagnoses is blocked before anyone sees it.

Urgency can only go up

Danger signs come from fixed rules. AI may add a second opinion that raises priority; lowering it is a recorded staff decision.

AI output is a labelled draft

Every sentence cites the answers it rests on. The doctor accepts, edits or rejects. AI never writes the doctor's notes.

Encrypted, hospital by hospital

Names, phones, case sheets, transcripts and paper photos are encrypted. Row-level security keeps each hospital's data apart.

Consent, purpose by purpose

Care consent is required; AI help is optional with nothing pre-selected, and the patient can stop it from their own phone.

Every read is audited

Who opened which record, when, and every edit as a new version. Earlier visits are visible only to the doctor caring for her today.
