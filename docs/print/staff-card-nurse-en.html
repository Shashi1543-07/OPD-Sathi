OPD Saathi · nurse's card

OPD Saathi takes the patient's history at the kiosk and keeps the queue. It never diagnoses. Patients come first; the screen comes second.

  1. Sign in

    Open https://<server>/staff on the desk computer with your own email and password. The first time, choose your own password. Never share an account.

  2. Urgent alerts come first

    A red alert at the top of Queue means a patient tapped a danger sign or asked for help. Go to the patient now, then press Acknowledge. Keep Alert sound on.

  3. Check what the kiosk cannot

    Nurse to confirm: confirm the danger sign in person. To change a patient's place, open ⋯ → Change priority, give a reason, then Apply override. If the department is not confirmed, type a reason and press Confirm department.

  4. Calling patients

    Call next calls the next patient in calling order, urgent first. If they do not come, wait for the countdown, then Mark absent. They move to Missed calls; Recall puts them back in their place with the same token.

  5. Patients who cannot use the kiosk

    Patients → Assisted registration: read the consent notice with the patient first, then enter their answers with them.

  6. Lost OPD card

    Patients → Lost OPD card: find the patient, check their age and sex with them, then Re-issue card. Give them the new PIN; the old one stops working.

  7. If the system is down

    Use the paper pack at the desk: the paper intake form and the paper token roll (P-001 …). Enter those visits once the system is back.
