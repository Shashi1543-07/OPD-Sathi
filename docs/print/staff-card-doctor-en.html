OPD Saathi · doctor's card

The kiosk takes the history in the patient's own words. It never diagnoses; every clinical decision is yours.

  1. Sign in

    Open https://<server>/staff with your own email and password. The first time, choose your own password.

  2. Call your next patient

    In Queue, Call next calls the next patient, urgent first. Then Open case sheet and Start consultation.

  3. Read what the patient said

    Overview shows the complaint, red flags and history; Interview transcript has every answer as given. Anything the patient or a nurse changed is marked.

  4. AI drafts are only drafts

    Check a draft before using it: tap a source to see the answer it rests on, then Accept, Edit or Reject. AI never diagnoses or prescribes.

  5. Your own notes

    Clinical notes: examination findings, assessment, plan and follow-up date, then Save notes. Each save is a new version, printed on the case sheet.

  6. Old papers

    Old papers: photos the patient brought. Accept or correct each reading before it counts as a result.

  7. Finish

    Print / PDF for the case sheet, then Finish consultation. If the patient does not come: Mark absent.

  8. Keyboard

    N: Go to the queue · E: Edit the first history field · P: Print the case sheet
