OPD Saathi · administrator's card

You keep OPD Saathi safe to use: who can sign in, which kiosks work, which features are on, and that backups run.

  1. Sign in safely

    Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app is required. Keep the ten recovery codes locked away. My account shows where you are signed in.

  2. Staff accounts

    Settings → Users → Create staff account: one account per person, with their department. Use Reset password for a forgotten password and Sign out everywhere when someone leaves or loses a device.

  3. Kiosks and phone check-in

    Kiosks → Register kiosk, then open its setup link once on the kiosk. Phone check-in QR: print it for the waiting area.

  4. Switches

    Departments: Patient interview, Online AI help and Features for patients. Turn a feature on only once the hospital has approved it.

  5. Every morning

    System: database, Redis and worker up, the last backup within a day, enough disk space. Alerts also reach IT by email.

  6. Who did what

    Audit log: sign-ins, record views and changes, with who and when.

  7. Reports

    Reports: visits, waiting times and AI use, without patient names.

  8. When something fails

    The runbooks in docs/runbooks say what to do for each alert. If the server is down in OPD hours, the desk switches to the paper pack.
