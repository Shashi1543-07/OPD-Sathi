## Who is responsible

This hospital is responsible for your information under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. It uses OPD Saathi to take your history before you see the doctor and to call you in turn.

## What we collect

- Your age, sex and preferred language. Your name only if a nurse registers you at the desk.
- Your answers about your health: your main problem, since when and how bad it is, other symptoms, medicines, allergies and earlier illnesses, and anything you type or say.
- The danger signs you tap, and photos of old papers if you choose to add them.
- Your mobile number, only if you give it. It helps staff find your earlier records, and is used for queue messages only if you tick the SMS box.
- Your OPD card number, the times of your visit, and who answered: you, a parent or guardian, or someone helping you.

## Why we use it

- To prepare your visit for the doctor and nurses and to call you in the right order. This is needed to check in.
- Online AI help, only if you agree: to turn speech into text, translate, read your papers and draft a summary for the doctor. You can check in without it.
- Queue messages by SMS, only if you tick the box.

OPD Saathi does not diagnose or prescribe. Your information is not sold or used for advertising.

## Who can see it

The doctors and nurses of the department that treats you, and the hospital's administrators where their work needs it. Every view of your record is logged. If you agree to online AI help, the AI service processes your answers only to return its result.

## Where it is kept, and for how long

On the hospital's own server, encrypted. It is kept for as long as the hospital's medical-record rules require, then deleted. A number given for queue messages is deleted after the day of your visit.

## Your choices and rights

- Stop online AI help or SMS messages at any time, from your token page or at the desk.
- Ask the hospital to show you your information, to correct it, or to delete what the law does not require it to keep.
- Name a person who may use these rights for you if you cannot.
- If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, contact the hospital's grievance officer (below). If their answer does not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
